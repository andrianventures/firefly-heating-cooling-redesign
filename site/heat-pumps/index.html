THE SHORT VERSION

Heat Pumps in Columbus, OH

A heat pump moves heat rather than generating it in the same way as a conventional furnace. The suitability of a particular setup depends on the building, existing equipment and installation design.

Firefly lists heat pumps on its published site. If you are evaluating a heat pump, ask about cold-weather performance, backup heat, electrical requirements and compatibility with your existing ducts.

If you already have a heat pump with poor heating or cooling, note the outdoor conditions and any displayed fault codes before calling.
