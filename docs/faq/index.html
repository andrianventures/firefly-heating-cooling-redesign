MORE ANSWERS

Good to know.

Is a heat pump the same as an air conditioner?

A heat pump can move heat in either direction, providing both heating and cooling when designed for the building.

Can dirty filters affect heating and cooling?

A clogged filter can restrict airflow. Choose a filter compatible with the equipment and follow the manufacturer guidance.

Do you service Galloway and Hilliard?

Both appear in the published service-area list. Call with the exact address to confirm current coverage.

Are online enquiries suitable for emergencies?

No. Call directly for time-sensitive heating or AC problems.
